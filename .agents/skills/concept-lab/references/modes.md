# Modes

| Mode | Required input | Operator | Output | Insufficient input |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| explore | bounded question or intuition | conceptual map | distinctions and questions | ask |
| distill | source refs and scope | concept-extraction@1 | ConceptFragments | hold |
| formalize | fragment refs and scope | theory-synthesis@1 | branches, definitions, relations | branch/hold |
| form | selected branch and target module | formation-projection@1 | FormationPacket | ask/hold |
| trace | artifact or packet ref | formation-trace@1 | ReverseTrace | no_hit/hold |

Never hide a mode transition inside prose.
