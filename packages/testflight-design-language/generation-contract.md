# User-facing generation contract

Every generated Testflight interface must:

1. identify its product surface, workspace scope, and active design-language revision;
2. consume semantic tokens from this package rather than introducing a parallel palette or scale;
3. preserve visible distinctions among draft, proposed, held, partial, accepted, rejected,
   superseded, and failed states;
4. design loading, empty, error, hold, no-hit, and insufficient-authority states;
5. provide complete keyboard navigation, visible focus, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion;
6. use direct, grounded language and avoid fake certainty or inflated product claims;
7. keep product design semantics separate from customer or brand semantics;
8. document any new semantic token, component pattern, or raw-value exception as a proposal.

Surface tuning is deliberate: Brand Intake is warmer and more guided; Brand Lab is denser and
analytical; Creative Studio permits more spatial expression. All retain shared hierarchy, state
language, focus treatment, spacing rhythm, and authority cues.
