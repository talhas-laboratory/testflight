# Biotic newsletter agent and fundamental content strategy design

Date: 2026-09-08
Status: captured as a candidate strategy foundation

## Outcome

Create an isolated Biotic content-strategy child that can serve as the durable substrate for
newsletter ideation and later individualized creative generation. Preserve the founder's central
thesis: content relevance comes from the relational common ground among person, self, field of
engagement, brand, and product—not from product proximity alone.

## Selected approach

Use a canonical child container rather than a loose note or an immediate executable workflow. The
child holds a human-readable evolving manifest, a machine-readable relational model, a newsletter
agent charter, and an independent evaluation rubric. This separates internal strategy from the
parent's public research while making retrieval and provenance explicit.

The agent is strategy-defined but not runtime-implemented. It produces reviewable proposals and
has no publication authority. Audience beliefs, scientific claims, and health or safety claims
require evidence appropriate to their type.

## Model

The relational model represents a person, contextual self-concept, field of engagement, current
tension or question, practice, brand, product, content contribution, and evidence. A candidate
piece must identify the field, reader stake, contribution, Biotic's legitimate role, and evidence
status. Product context is optional.

Topic selection is judged on field centrality, lived stake, contribution value, brand legitimacy,
and reader independence. Hard gates prevent unsupported claims, exploitative identity framing,
epistemic collapse, and unauthorized publication.

## Future implementation boundary

A later runtime may compile the manifest and model with authorized audience and topic evidence,
research a bounded question, generate an issue packet, evaluate it independently, and submit it
for human review. Runtime registration, provider configuration, subscriber data access, sending,
and feedback ingestion are explicitly outside this design increment.

## Validation

Validate the child-container structure and the existing concrete Biotic workspace. Do not build or
populate a live Cognee projection until separately authorized. Retrieval certification remains a
future step because no staging projection has been built.
