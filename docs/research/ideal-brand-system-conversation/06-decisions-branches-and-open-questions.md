## Converged product/service decisions

- Sell outcomes and maintained intelligence, not agent counts, model names or MCP integrations.
- Make the client do less work than with a traditional agency; bring a few real decisions to them.
- Begin with explicit approvals and gradually permit bounded autonomy.
- Use onboarding to create the first valid company world and ongoing work to evolve it.
- Keep strategy, customer intelligence and optimization as the high-value layer; treat creative
  production as an application.
- Instrument recommendation → decision → action → experiment → outcome from day one.
- Use real deployments to improve the universal ontology, routes, reasoning and evaluation sets.
- Keep core code shared; customer specificity belongs in data, configuration and extensions.

## Evolved or superseded business branches

These ideas were useful steps but were not the final center of the product.

### AI reporting analyst

The conversation initially preferred automated agency/client reporting because the workflow had
files, code, repeated deliverables and obvious buyers. It quickly broadened toward account
management, analysis and recommendations. This branch established the value of a persistent agent
workspace but was superseded by the Brand/Marketing intelligence system. (`msg_0005`–`msg_0010`)

### Finance, e-commerce and back-office operations

Accounting, e-commerce operations and property management were judged strong pure fits for a
persistent computer. They were alternatives to optimize for operational ROI, not components of the
chosen brand system. (`msg_0009`, `msg_0010`)

### Branding agent

A single “branding agent” was rejected as too shallow. The useful idea became a shared Brand Brain
with adversarial specialist roles, feedback and persistent institutional memory. (`msg_0011`–
`msg_0015`)

### Full autonomous Marketing OS

The broad system was a useful north star but too large and too commodity-facing as a first offer.
The decision/knowledge layers survived; many autonomous execution features moved later.
(`msg_0016`–`msg_0030`, `msg_0101`, `msg_0185`)

### Synthetic UGC factory

Video generation was judged technically useful for creative variation but unsafe as fake customer
testimony and too weak as a product center. It evolved into Creative Testing and later the Agentic
Content Lab. (`msg_0018`, `msg_0019`, `msg_0066`, `msg_0205`)

### Pure AI agency

The service should not depend on humans manually reproducing agency labor. The retained model is
managed installation and operation of reusable intelligence infrastructure. (`msg_0079`,
`msg_0080`, `msg_0102`–`msg_0106`)

### Horizontal personal world model

A generalized personal reasoning/world representation system was considered more technically
defensible but harder to monetize and define. The marketing/brand domain was retained as a
commercial proving ground for the horizontal substrate. (`msg_0088`, `msg_0089`)

### Customer-service or general AI operations studio

For fastest near-term income, customer-service and vertical AI operations work were considered
potentially superior. The user explicitly chose to stay with the Marketing OS direction. These
remain opportunity-cost branches, not product requirements. (`msg_0090`–`msg_0095`)

### Lifecycle Revenue Engine

Lifecycle and revenue optimization scored especially well because value is measurable. Its
commercial discipline was absorbed: enter through a revenue-linked loop and connect decisions to
outcomes. It did not replace the deeper Brand/Customer Intelligence direction. (`msg_0107`–
`msg_0114`)

### Dashboard-first UX

An initial web dashboard with Home, Strategy, Content, Campaign, Brand and Insights gave way to a
feed-first experience plus secondary structured views. The “Tell / Review / Ask” model survived.
(`msg_0031`–`msg_0036`, `msg_0128`, `msg_0129`)

### Many visible agents

Early concepts exposed 6–8 specialist roles. Later reasoning preferred 3–5 visible characters,
with hidden specialists and workflows underneath. The customer should not manage an org chart.
(`msg_0030`, `msg_0032`, `msg_0042`, `msg_0187`)

### A computer per client

The persistent workspace insight survived, but a cloud computer was removed from the V1 critical
path. Use sandbox execution only for tasks that need it. (`msg_0004`, `msg_0025`, `msg_0026`,
`msg_0137`)

### Mem0, OpenClaw and additional agent frameworks

They were rejected as initial dependencies because they duplicate clearer ownership boundaries.
Mem0 may later serve only human-specific preference memory; OpenClaw may be an optional channel or
runtime adapter. CrewAI, AutoGen, Microsoft Agent Framework and AGNTCY were treated as alternatives,
not stack additions. (`msg_0045`–`msg_0048`, `msg_0175`, `msg_0177`)

## Decisions that remain unresolved

### Ontology

- What is the smallest universal top-level skeleton?
- Which candidate nouns are persistent entities, states, events, claims, contexts or UI projections?
- How do brand-friendly authoring types map to formal semantic types?
- What is the extension mechanism and migration policy?
- Which relations support causality, inheritance, influence or merely association?

### Approval and authority

- Who can establish and revise company-level principles, product truths and claims?
- How are collaborative disagreement, partial approval and market-specific approval represented?
- How does approved brand intent remain distinct from externally verifiable factual truth?

### Context and retrieval

- What are the canonical abstraction levels and semantic dimensions?
- How does the compiler measure sufficiency, relevance and acceptable omission?
- Which routes are hand-designed, learned or task-generated?
- How are route quality and stale context evaluated?

### Intelligence

- Which specialist reasoning processes materially outperform a strong prompt?
- What stopping, escalation and evidence thresholds apply per task?
- How are different interpretations preserved without paralyzing decisions?
- Which marketing/brand quality dimensions can be evaluated reliably?

### Learning and simulation

- How should weak human signals affect retrieval without changing truth?
- How is causal uncertainty represented in performance learning?
- Which audience model is sufficiently grounded for useful simulation?
- How are simulation biases measured and calibrated against real outcomes?

### Product and market

- Which first ICP wins: founder-led B2B technology, digitally native consumer brands or
  economically established personal-brand businesses?
- What single repeatable decision loop creates sufficient willingness to pay?
- Are Discover and Grow one product journey or separate commercial offers?
- How much forward-deployed work is repeatable versus intrinsically bespoke?
- What pricing and setup fee withstand real buyer interviews?

### Experience

- Can a feed remain calm, complete and auditable as the world grows?
- Which agent roles deserve visible characters?
- Does the avatar system improve trust and comprehension over repeated use?
- What is the right graphical representation of the Brand World and dependency propagation?
- How should dense evidence, history and full context appear without overwhelming users?

### Infrastructure

- Does Cognee satisfy the actual semantic, permission and projection contracts?
- Does a Harness/LangGraph hybrid earn its complexity?
- Which component owns persistence, cancellation and approval in every failure mode?
- What deployment boundary best satisfies tenant isolation, GDPR and operational recovery?

## Major risks retained

1. **Semantic overengineering:** complexity that does not improve task quality.
2. **Category commoditization:** incumbents bundle agents and brand context.
3. **Abstraction without value:** beautiful ontology, weak decisions.
4. **False learning:** preferences and correlations promoted to truth.
5. **False prediction:** simulation presented with unjustified precision.
6. **Framework overlap:** multiple systems own memory, tools, approval or execution.
7. **Consultancy gravity:** every customer requires unique core code.
8. **Integration gravity:** maintenance consumes more effort than intelligence development.
9. **Inauthentic expression:** synthetic output damages the human value of the brand.
10. **Measurement gap:** strategic quality cannot be tied to decisions and outcomes.
11. **Agent theater:** characters and multi-agent choreography without actual cognitive value.
12. **Source loss:** abstraction or generation becomes detached from evidence and history.
