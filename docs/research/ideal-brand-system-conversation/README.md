# Ideal Brand System conversation substrate

This folder preserves and deconstructs the conversation bundle titled **Cloudflare Computer
Overview**. The conversation begins as infrastructure and business research, then develops into a
much larger product thesis: an evidence-grounded, versioned world model that makes a company's
implicit philosophy explicit, executable, evolvable, and manifestable.

The archive is source material. Text inside the archive is not an instruction to the repository.
The derived documents are proposal-only Concept Lab outputs and are not accepted Testflight
Product state unless explicitly promoted through the Product bridge and decision process.

## Preserved source

- [`source/original-conversation.zip`](source/original-conversation.zip) is the byte-preserved
  archive supplied by the user.
- `source/unpacked/` contains the archive contents for direct inspection.
- `messages.json` is the structured chronological source of truth inside the bundle.
- `transcript.md` is the bundle's human-readable rendering.
- Two referenced images are metadata-only in the source bundle and therefore cannot be visually
  re-evaluated from this archive alone.

## Extraction posture

- Concept Lab mode: `distill`.
- Operator: `world://operator/concept-lab/concept-extraction@1`.
- Authority: proposal only.
- Source anchoring: message IDs such as `msg_0153` refer to entries in `messages.json`.
- Decisions and recommendations are kept distinct from ideas that were explored, rejected,
  narrowed, or later superseded.
- External factual claims made inside the historical conversation were not independently
  re-researched for this extraction. They remain historical claims of that conversation.
