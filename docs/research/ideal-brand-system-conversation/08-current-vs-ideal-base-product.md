# Current Testflight versus the ideal base product

## Status and scope

This is a proposal-only comparison. It compares:

- the **current accepted Testflight Product state** represented by the Product workspace and the
  repository's tested implementation slices; and
- the **ideal base product** distilled from the preserved conversation.

It does not promote the conversation into accepted Product truth. A repository file counts as
evidence only for the maturity its own lifecycle declares: a schema is not a workflow, a fixture is
not a customer world, a prototype is not a production surface, and a passing test is not market
validation.

## Executive conclusion

The current project is **a strong lower-layer foundation for the ideal, but it is not yet the ideal
base product**.

The build order is inverted:

- Testflight has built much of the semantic kernel, authority model, provider isolation, context
  packet machinery, workflow runtime, Creative Field, and local inspection tooling.
- The conversation's base product is the coherent loop above those pieces: construct one approved
  company world, compile task-specific context, let recognizable specialists make a consequential
  recommendation or artifact, let a human inspect and change it, execute an approved action, and
  learn from the result.

The current Product workspace still names the active product as the **Testflight integration
laboratory**. The Founder Brand World is proposed, and the initial commercial wedge remains
unresolved between World Seeder and Campaign Decision Intelligence. That is the clearest sign that
the repository contains components of the intended product without yet having accepted the same
base-product identity as the conversation.

The rubric score is **37 / 80 (46%)**. This is a maturity index, not a percentage of engineering
work completed. Roughly stated:

- semantic and infrastructure substrate: strong;
- isolated end-to-end slices: credible;
- unified product experience and operating loop: weak;
- customer and commercial proof: absent.

## The two base products in plain language

### Current base product

An integration laboratory and semantic platform composed of:

- a provider-neutral semantic kernel;
- a Brand ontology and isolated Brand workspaces;
- rebuildable retrieval projections;
- a task-scoped context intelligence engine;
- versioned reasoning operators and declarative workflows;
- local Brand Intake, Brand Lab, Brand World, and Creative Studio surfaces;
- local authenticated workflow and operator runtimes;
- a tested Biotic newsletter workflow and social-carousel workflow family.

Its center of gravity is **correct representation, safe boundaries, and composable technical
capabilities**.

### Ideal base product from the conversation

A managed brand-intelligence product that:

1. constructs and maintains an approved, evidence-aware model of one company;
2. continuously receives evidence from the company and its environment;
3. uses a Context Compiler to give each specialist exactly the relevant world slice;
4. produces typed insights, recommendations, decisions, and artifacts;
5. lets humans inspect evidence, discuss, adjust, approve, reject, or branch them;
6. externalizes approved meaning into work and artifacts;
7. observes consequences and updates the world without confusing performance with causality.

Its center of gravity is **better consequential decisions and coherent manifestations**, delivered
through a calm human relationship with an artificial organization.

## What should be preserved

The current project has several advantages over the conversation's original architecture sketch:

1. **It is more epistemically disciplined.** Proposal-only mutation, evidence/truth separation,
   authority grants, branch preservation, no-hit/hold behavior, and derivation receipts are already
   explicit rather than aspirational.
2. **It has better provider boundaries.** Cognee, LangGraph, MCP, Harness, model providers, SQLite,
   and creative targets sit behind owned interfaces.
3. **It has executable proof, not just diagrams.** The Biotic newsletter workflow preserves claim
   lineage through composition, plan, generation packet, draft, evaluation, and human-review
   package. Creative Studio also proves a governed external-action seam.
4. **It already separates customer worlds from Product truth.** This is necessary for a reusable
   multi-brand platform and was underspecified in the historical conversation.
5. **Its design principle is compatible with the ideal.** Calm density, evidence before decoration,
   visible state, and progressive disclosure are the correct foundation for the future feed.

These are core assets. The recommended direction is **composition and productization, not a clean
rewrite**.

## The principal gaps

### 1. No single accepted product center

The repository simultaneously carries an active integration-lab identity, a proposed founder-world
product, and a research-recommended campaign-intelligence wedge. The ideal can support both wedges,
but the base product needs one accepted first decision loop.

### 2. The Brand World is modeled more as governed knowledge than organizational philosophy

The current ontology is strong at record correctness and marketing-domain structure. It is weaker
at representing the deepest ideal: beliefs, tensions, identity continuity, generative principles,
meaning transformation, and the relationship between internal reality and outward manifestation.

### 3. The human loop is fragmented

Brand Intake, Brand Lab, Brand World, Creative Studio, and Workflow Runtime demonstrate different
parts of the experience, but the user cannot yet move through one coherent path:

```text
signal → insight → recommendation → discussion → adjustment → approval
       → work/artifact → external action → outcome → learning
```

### 4. Context compilation is not yet route intelligence

The compiler can build a bounded evidence packet. It does not yet choose and evaluate a reusable
route through the world based on a complete agent, task, state, authority, sufficiency, and cost
model.

### 5. Agents are workflow callers, not an artificial organization

The system has operator procedures and workflow principals, but not durable specialist definitions,
visible responsibilities, shared work references, escalation relationships, or a Director.

### 6. Manifestation remains vertical-specific

Newsletter and carousel workflows can create bounded artifacts, and Creative Field can govern a
creative project. The universal transformation from brand principle to channel-specific language,
visual, motion, product, spatial, or event instructions is not present.

### 7. Learning is correctly typed but empty

The project has excellent contracts for learning without false causality. It has no actual customer
feedback or outcome stream to make those contracts operational.

## Semantic and structural tensions to resolve

- **Ontology v1 versus v2:** the eight intuitive systems are excellent navigation, while v2's
  modules are stronger semantic ownership boundaries. Treat the eight systems as a world-view
  projection over modular canonical types, not as a competing ontology.
- **Brand record versus navigation record:** system labels, dossiers, sources, claims,
  interpretations, and gaps must remain separate types. Navigation convenience must not become
  domain truth.
- **Research world versus approved client world:** the Biotic public-evidence workspace proves
  research handling, not the future FDE-authored and client-approved Biotic Brand World.
- **Operator versus agent:** an operator is a reusable cognitive procedure. An agent selects and
  applies operators under an identity, role, responsibility, knowledge scope, and tool policy.
- **Workflow versus work:** a workflow definition schedules computation. A durable Task or Decision
  is a domain object that can outlive, span, or invoke several workflows.
- **Context packet versus route:** a packet is the result of one compile. A route is the inspectable,
  reusable policy that explains how that packet was assembled and whether the path worked.
- **Representation rule versus Manifestation Engine:** existing rules express constraints. The
  engine must perform and evaluate the contextual transformation of meaning.

## What is overbuilt relative to the missing base loop

“Overbuilt” here means sequenced ahead of proof, not useless.

- Several sophisticated semantic capabilities—federation, branch overlays, dependency propagation,
  representation profiles, and Creative Field orchestration—exist before one customer has completed
  the base discovery-to-decision loop.
- Four separate user-facing surfaces exist before a unified product shell and feed.
- Multiple runtime and provider adapters exist before one accepted ICP and one measured workflow.
- Two Brand ontology organizations coexist before the product has resolved how canonical semantic
  modules project into a simple user and agent map.
- The public Biotic world is deeply modeled in places, while founder/client approval and ongoing
  world maintenance are still prototypes.

The sequencing correction is to stop expanding breadth until the existing pieces form one useful,
measured loop.

## Missing end-to-end loops

### Required base loop

```text
FDE/client discovery
→ bounded source ingestion
→ evidence and interpretation
→ provisional Brand World
→ contradiction and coverage review
→ client approval into current state
→ specialist task
→ task/agent/state/authority context compile
→ typed recommendation
→ evidence drill-down and discussion
→ adjust/approve/reject/branch
→ durable work or artifact
→ authorized externalization
→ outcome evidence
→ reviewed learning and staleness propagation
```

No current application exercises this complete sequence.

### Strongest existing partial loop

The Biotic newsletter workflow currently proves:

```text
bounded supplied evidence
→ relational composition alternatives
→ content plan
→ generation packet
→ draft
→ independent evaluation
→ proposal-only human review package
```

It is the best candidate to become the first base-product proof because it already carries semantic
lineage and clear review boundaries. It is not yet connected to accepted Brand World authoring,
the shared Context Compiler, feed interaction, artifact persistence across the general Work Model,
publication approval, or outcome learning.

## Recommended target architecture

Keep the existing packages and organize them into six product planes:

1. **World plane** — canonical Brand records, evidence, decisions, versions, dependencies, and a
   stable Brand World API. SQLite remains the first canonical adapter; Cognee remains a projection.
2. **Compilation plane** — one Context Compiler contract accepting agent, task, current work state,
   authority, desired abstraction, and sufficiency policy. Routes become versioned definitions with
   evaluations and trace receipts.
3. **Cognition plane** — versioned specialist definitions that compose existing reasoning operators.
   Begin with Researcher, Strategist, and Brand Guardian; add Creator through the newsletter slice.
4. **Work plane** — a generic append-oriented Work Ledger for Task, Result/Artifact, Event,
   Dependency, Status, Decision, review, and domain references. Existing workflow runs and Creative
   Field runs become execution receipts linked to work, not the work model itself.
5. **Experience plane** — one product shell centered on typed feed objects, anchored conversation,
   evidence drill-down, adjustment, approval, history, and secondary Brand World/Work views. Reuse
   the active design language and the Brand World canvas.
6. **Externalization and learning plane** — authority-gated connectors, artifact/manifester
   workflows, outcome ingestion, reviewed learning, and dependency-based staleness.

The first deployment should keep source connectors and analytical infrastructure deliberately
small. Manual FDE population is acceptable because it is part of the proposed service model.

## Staged migration plan

### Stage 0 — decide the base-product contract

- Accept one product identity and one first ICP/workflow.
- Recommended choice: an FDE-managed Brand World with the Biotic newsletter as the first visible
  decision/artifact loop.
- Define success as decision quality, evidence traceability, review burden, correction rate, time to
  approved artifact, and downstream usefulness—not number of agents or records.

### Stage 1 — reconcile the world model

- Make ontology v2 modules canonical and the eight systems an intuitive projection/view.
- Add the missing philosophy/identity-continuity and manifestation concepts as proposals.
- Define the minimum approved Brand World profile required by the newsletter loop.
- Populate a new approved-client Biotic branch separately from the public-research workspace.

### Stage 2 — complete Brand World authoring

- Connect Brand Intake to canonical evidence/proposal persistence.
- Add FDE interpretation, contradiction, coverage, clarification, and client approval queues.
- Expose history, source context, and explicit unknown/not-applicable states.

### Stage 3 — promote Context Compiler v2

- Add agent ID/version, Task reference, current state, AuthorityGrant, desired abstraction, and
  sufficiency policy to the request.
- Persist RouteDefinition and RouteEvaluation separately from ContextPacket and ContextRunTrace.
- Prove high-level-first deepening and record why expansion stopped.

### Stage 4 — compose the first specialist loop

- Define reusable Researcher, Strategist/Newsletter Strategist, Creator, and Brand Guardian agents.
- Make their cognition explicit compositions of current operators and deterministic validators.
- Link the existing newsletter workflow to the shared compiler and accepted Brand World references.

### Stage 5 — add the Work Ledger and unified feed

- Introduce Task, Result/Artifact, Event, Dependency, Status, Decision, Discussion, and Review.
- Translate runtime events into stable domain events.
- Build only the initial post types: Insight, Recommendation, Approval Request, and Result.
- Mount Brand World canvas, evidence dossier, discussion, and history as progressive disclosure from
  those objects.

### Stage 6 — close the real-world loop

- Add explicit editorial approval and one publication/export connector.
- Ingest bounded outcome and qualitative feedback as evidence.
- Produce reviewed LearningUpdates and dependency/staleness proposals.
- Run the complete loop repeatedly before adding simulation or broad source integrations.

### Stage 7 — expand proprietary intelligence

- Generalize Manifestation Policies and Artifact Composition Graphs from the newsletter/Creative
  Field evidence.
- Add simulation only when real outcomes exist for calibration.
- Expand to events, product behavior, spatial experience, and richer multi-agent coordination only
  after the base loop is measurably useful.

## Evaluation fixtures required for promotion

| Capability | Minimum promotion fixture |
| --- | --- |
| Approved Brand World | Founder/FDE evidence produces a provisional world; client corrections alter proposals; only authorized approval changes current state. |
| Ontology projection | The same canonical records appear correctly in modular API, eight-system map, and task context without becoming duplicate entities. |
| Context Compiler v2 | Two different agents/tasks receive different sufficient packets from the same world; every inclusion, exclusion, conflict, and stop decision is traceable. |
| Specialist cognition | Strategist produces alternatives and disconfirming evidence; Brand Guardian catches a seeded unsupported claim; both hold on missing authority. |
| Work Ledger | A recommendation survives retry, reassignment, review, cancellation, and resume without duplicated actions or lost references. |
| Feed | A user can inspect evidence, discuss, adjust, approve, reject, and revisit history without opening a runtime log or ontology editor. |
| Newsletter loop | Approved Brand World → compiled context → draft → guardian review → human approval → export, with exact claim and context continuity. |
| Learning | A real outcome becomes evidence and a reviewed learning proposal; it does not automatically rewrite strategy. |
| Staleness | An accepted upstream Brand change marks only context-overlapping dependent artifacts for review and explains why. |
| Cross-brand reuse | A second brand uses the same platform contracts, agents, routes, and UI while keeping its world, extensions, sources, and permissions isolated. |
