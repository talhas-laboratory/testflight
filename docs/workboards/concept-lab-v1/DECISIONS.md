# Decisions

Record durable decisions here.

## 2026-09-08 — Use a skill-first, repository-native v1

Concept Lab v1 will use a repo-local Codex skill, canonical files, portable reasoning operators,
and deterministic validation. It will not add a dedicated app, persistent mode runtime, model
provider, database, or live retrieval projection.

## 2026-09-08 — Keep Concept Lab and Testflight Product sovereign

Concept Lab owns speculative theory and formation history. Testflight Product owns product
definition, architecture, research, and decisions. They exchange immutable inquiry and formation
packets through explicit, non-traversable-by-default bridges.

## 2026-09-08 — Require human promotion

Every Lab-to-Product output has `canonical_write: false`. Promotion requires explicit human
authority even when structural, semantic, and usefulness evaluators pass.

## 2026-09-08 — Use Biotic only as the first proof case

The relational-content theory and existing Biotic founder brief will test forward formation and
reverse tracing. Their concepts must not silently become universal Concept Lab ontology or
accepted customer/scientific facts.
