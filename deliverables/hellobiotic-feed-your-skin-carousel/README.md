## Caption

Sweat gets the blame, but body odor is more of a bacteria story. Your underarms are home to a microbiome: a living community on your skin. When odor-causing bacteria become more noticeable, things can get a little pungent.

Biotic's Brightening Probiotic Deodorant is made with pre-, pro-, and postbiotics and is positioned by the brand as a microbiome-friendly way to neutralize odor-causing bacteria. Feed your skin something good. ☀️
