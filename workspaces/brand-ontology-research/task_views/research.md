# Research view

Use this view for source discovery and bounded synthesis. Register every source with language,
authority tier, URL, date, and exact evidence spans. Extract candidate concepts; do not write
accepted ontology definitions or Brand-world facts here.

Route through `ontology-conflation` when a source term could be a type, property, assertion, event,
or governance object. Preserve `partial`, `hold`, and `no_hit` outcomes.
