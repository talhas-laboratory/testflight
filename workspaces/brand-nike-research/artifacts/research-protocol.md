# Nike public-brand research protocol

This workspace is an isolated research branch. It proposes a public-evidence representation of the
Nike Brand; it does not define Nike's canonical identity and does not authorize asset publication.

## Population method

1. Bound the subject to the Nike Brand inside NIKE, Inc. Jordan Brand and Converse remain related
   portfolio brands and are not merged into Nike identity.
2. Prefer regulated filings and Nike primary sources for Nike's own mission, strategy, offerings,
   campaigns, events, and governance. Use independent sources for external perception. Use each
   competitor's own reporting only for that competitor's self-positioning.
3. Save a bounded excerpt and a paraphrase, plus actor authority, perspective, epistemic status,
   time, context, and limitations. Full source copies are not stored.
4. Propose entities under all eight Brand ontology types and relationships under all fourteen
   relation types. Absence of evidence produces `held`, `partial`, or `no_hit`, not invention.
5. Keep campaign, audience, channel, geography, and time scopes attached. Do not lift a local
   expression into a universal Nike rule.
6. Validate references and ontology compatibility locally. Only a separately certified staging
   projection may be populated into Cognee; canonical promotion is prohibited.

## Acceptance and refresh

An item is retrievable as a proposal only when every evidence and context reference resolves. A
`held` relation may be returned as a hypothesis if its limitation is included. Refresh sources at
least every 90 days and after a material strategy event, annual filing, or independent perception
update. Rebuild generated projections from these tracked artifacts rather than editing runtime
state.

## Applied reasoning operators

- `workspace-isolation@1`: separates this branch and staging dataset from canonical Brand state.
- `identity-provenance-conflation@1`: keeps NIKE, Inc., Nike, Jordan, Converse, sources, and
  assertions distinct.
- `evidence-truth-conflation@1`: prevents Nike or competitor self-description from becoming
  unqualified truth.
- `retrieval-reasoning-conflation@1`: keeps retrieved evidence distinct from the agent's synthesis.
- `validation-persistence-conflation@1`: requires validation before any runtime write.
- `version-state-conflation@1`: preserves research cutoff and valid-time context.
