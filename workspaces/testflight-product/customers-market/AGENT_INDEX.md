# Customers and market

Route users, customers, organizations, segments, needs, jobs, journeys, feedback, research,
competitors, and market context here. Separate observed evidence, participant statements,
interpretations, hypotheses, and accepted product decisions.

Personal or confidential data requires an explicit policy and permission. Return `NO_HITS` rather
than inferring customer facts from product intent.

`actor-market-inventory.yaml` contains roles and segment hypotheses—not named customers.
`journey-inventory.yaml` contains designed journeys—not observed behavior.
