# Experience design

Route all user-facing language, aesthetics, typography, color, spacing, motion, components,
interaction patterns, accessibility, and cross-surface coherence here. Load
`artifacts/design-language.yaml` before generating or changing a user-facing experience and use
`packages/testflight-design-language` in implementation.

Customer brand language remains isolated and may enter only as an authorized, scoped overlay.

Use `artifacts/surface-pattern-inventory.yaml` to compare the three implemented surfaces. It
records observed patterns and current token-adoption drift separately from the active contract.
