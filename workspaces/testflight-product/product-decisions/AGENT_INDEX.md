# Product decisions

Route here for proposals, alternatives, trade-offs, decisions, authority, review, and
supersession. A proposal is never an accepted decision. Every material decision preserves the
rejected alternatives, affected modules, evidence, valid time, and authority.

Use exact or BM25 retrieval first. Reject this index as a domain answer.

Start with `artifacts/decision-catalog.yaml`; follow each decision to its original ADR, workboard
decision log, or accepted product-infrastructure decision.
